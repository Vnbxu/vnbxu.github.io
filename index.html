Biography

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at Sun Yat-sen University, working under the guidance of Prof. Deke Guo. My research broadly focuses on AI for Networking, with particular emphasis on integrating AI into network infrastructure and management.

Prior to my Ph.D. program, I received my B.S. degree from Sun Yat-sen University and began my graduate research under the mentorship of Assoc. Prof. Xiaoxi Zhang. I have also worked as a Research Intern at Alibaba Cloud with Dr. Ennan Zhai and Dr. Xuan Zeng, exploring AI-driven network operations.
